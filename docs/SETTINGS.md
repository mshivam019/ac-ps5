# Controls and settings

Default controls: Cross=A, Circle=B, Square=X, Triangle=Y, Options=Start,
L2=L, R2=R, R1=Z. The SDL Back/touchpad binding opens the port menu where
supported; title-screen Options also opens settings.

The 2160p60 release renders at 3840x2160 with a 60 FPS target. Measured scene
windows range roughly 30–60 FPS. Resolution, VSync, frame cap and MSAA follow
the built PS5 profile. The menu retains texture filtering, audio and gameplay
settings; HD textures use on-demand loading.

Settings and saves live in the title sandbox under `/download0`, not beside
the executable. Preserve these during updates. Save by speaking to the gyroid
outside your house and choosing Save and Quit; wait for completion.
